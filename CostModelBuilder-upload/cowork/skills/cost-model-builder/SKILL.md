# Cost Model Builder (Cowork helper)

The Cost Model Builder is a managed app at **<APP URL>** (replace with the live URL from `ms app play`). It prices the tender with a tested engine, walks the seller through the questions, runs the approval checks and builds the workbook. This skill gets the seller there with everything in place. It does not price anything.

## When the seller asks to cost a tender

1. **Find the tender pack.** Ask which client and opportunity it is if that isn't clear. Look for the pack in the seller's recent email attachments, Teams chats and SharePoint. Typical files: the cleaning specification (PDF or DOCX), a site list or store schedule (XLSX or CSV), pricing schedules, TUPE lists and trading hours.
2. **Put it in the opportunity folder.** Propose saving the files to the bids library as `Bids/<Client>/<Opportunity name>/Tender pack/`. Show the list of files and wait for the seller to approve before you copy anything.
3. **Check the pack is complete.** Tell the seller, in a short list, which of these you found and which are missing:
   - the site list, with a floor area or weekly hours for each site;
   - the cleaning specification: tasks, frequencies and service levels;
   - the contract term and start date;
   - TUPE: whether staff transfer, and the employee list if they do;
   - trading or access hours;
   - any client pricing template or schedule of rates.
   Name the document for each item you found. If something is missing, say so and suggest the seller asks the client. Don't guess.
4. **Hand over to the app.** Give the seller the app link and say: "Open the Cost Model Builder, choose Load the tender pack, and add the files from the Tender pack folder. The Guide button explains every question as you go."

## Rules

- **Never give a price, weekly charge, hourly rate, margin, discount or bid/no-bid view**, even if asked or even if a figure appears in the documents. Say: "The Cost Model Builder works that out, and your approver signs it off."
- Never compare with other clients, other bids or other companies' figures.
- Don't summarise or forward the tender documents outside the opportunity folder and this conversation.
- Ask before sending any email or Teams message, and before copying or moving files.

## After the workbook is built

If the seller asks, draft the approval request to the approvers the app named (bid manager and commercial manager, plus commercial director or finance director where the app says so). Include the client, the opportunity, the link to the workbook in the opportunity folder and the approval level the app showed. Show the draft and wait for the seller to approve it before sending.
